2. Scope

This privacy policy covers:

  • this website (hosted on GitHub Pages),
  • email support for Evictio,
  • the game Evictio on Steam and — once released there — on the Epic Games Store, PlayStation and Xbox.

Your Steam account, Epic Games account or console account is controlled by Valve, Epic Games, Sony or Microsoft. Their terms and privacy policies apply in addition to this notice. We are not the controller of those platform accounts.

3. What we do not do

  • no player account or login operated by us
  • no advertising, no AdMob, no marketing pixels
  • no game servers of our own and no online multiplayer
  • no Unity Analytics, Unreal Analytics, Sentry or similar third-party analytics / crash tools
  • no precise location data
  • no sale of personal data
  • no automated decision-making and no profiling under Article 22 GDPR

4. Local game data

Save files and settings are stored locally on your device. We do not receive those files unless you attach them to a support email.

Steam Cloud is not currently used. If that changes, this section will be updated.

5. Platform services (Steam, later Epic / consoles)

Evictio may use the official platform SDK so store features work as advertised:

  • Steam: launch through Steam, ownership check, Steam Overlay, and Steam Achievements when you complete in-game goals.
  • Epic Games Store (planned): storefront distribution. Epic Achievements / Epic Online Services are not enabled at the time of this notice. This section will be updated if that changes.
  • PlayStation / Xbox (planned): trophies or Xbox Achievements and platform save features are handled by Sony or Microsoft.

The platform typically processes an account identifier (for example a Steam ID), playtime, progress and unlocked achievements. Whether those achievements appear on your public profile is controlled in that platform’s privacy settings.

We do not receive your email address or real name from the platform merely because you play Evictio.

Relevant platform documents:

  • Steam Privacy Policy and Subscriber Agreement
  • Epic Games Privacy Policy and Store EULA
  • PlayStation / Xbox: the current Sony or Microsoft privacy policy and terms of use

6. Email support

If you contact us, we process the information you send, which may include:

  • email address and name, if you provide them
  • message content, screenshots, logs and save files
  • technical details you include (operating system, hardware, error text)
  • a Steam / Epic / console display name, if you include it

Purpose: handling your request. Legal bases: Article 6(1)(b) GDPR (support related to your purchase of the game) and Article 6(1)(f) GDPR (our legitimate interest in providing understandable player support). We do not use support emails for advertising.

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., United States). Visiting the site creates ordinary technical server logs, which may include IP address, date and time, requested file and browser user agent. This processing is required to deliver the page (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

We do not set our own cookies on this website and we do not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel or similar advertising / analytics tools here.

GitHub may process log data in the United States. GitHub participates in the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Standard Contractual Clauses may also apply.

Depending on how you use the game and this site, recipients may include:

  • Valve / Steam, and later Epic Games, Sony or Microsoft — for the store, accounts and platform features

Some of these organisations are in the United States or other countries outside the EEA. Transfers rely on an adequacy decision, the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and/or Standard Contractual Clauses (Articles 44 et seq. GDPR).

  • Platform achievements and store purchases: according to the platform operator’s rules.
  • Support emails: for as long as needed to handle the request, then normally no longer than 12 months, unless a longer period is required to establish, exercise or defend legal claims or to meet a statutory duty.
